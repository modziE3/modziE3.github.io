Research

Autocatalytic Networks

Python · Graph Theory · Random Networks · Simulation

Computational research into autocatalytic reaction systems and the conditions under which self-sustaining network structures emerge.

Research question

I investigated RAF-type autocatalytic networks in random catalytic reaction systems, including how their probability and structure change with the level of catalysis.

I implemented algorithms for identifying RAF structures and used large batches of simulations to study phase-transition-like behaviour and structural properties of the resulting networks.

Why I enjoyed it

The project sat directly between discrete mathematics, algorithms and computational science. It involved turning abstract mathematical definitions into efficient code, designing experiments, and interpreting patterns that only become visible at scale.

Scientific computing

Computational Fluid Dynamics

Python · Navier–Stokes · Numerical PDEs · Simulation

An incompressible fluid simulation project focused on visuals, speed and numerical stability.

View code

Numerical method

I implemented a 2D incompressible Navier–Stokes solver using operator splitting. The simulation combines semi-Lagrangian advection, implicit diffusion and a pressure projection step to enforce approximately divergence-free flow.

Engineering the simulation

A major focus was balancing stability, computational cost and visual quality. I experimented with timesteps, viscosity, iterative solvers and boundary treatment while producing animated dye-flow visualisations.
